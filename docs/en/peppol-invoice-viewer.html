Why it works: Peppol BIS is EN 16931

Peppol BIS Billing 3.0 is a CIUS (specification) of EN 16931, the European e-invoicing standard, using the UBL syntax. Any invoice that reached you via a Peppol access point is therefore readable here — as are German XRechnung files and hybrid Factur-X/ZUGFeRD PDFs (how those work).

Validate against the official rules

Need to know whether the invoice is actually compliant? The free EN 16931 validator runs the official rule sets in your browser and lists every violation with rule ID and location — again with no upload.

Privacy by architecture

Invoices contain bank details, prices and business data. This tool processes files locally in your browser: no upload, no server, no account. Close the tab and nothing remains.
