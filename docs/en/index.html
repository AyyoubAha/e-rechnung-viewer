What this tool does

XRechnung & UBL
Reads both official XML syntaxes (UBL and UN/CEFACT CII) used across the EU under EN 16931, including German XRechnung and Peppol BIS style invoices.
ZUGFeRD / Factur-X PDFs
Extracts and displays the machine-readable XML embedded in hybrid invoice PDFs, so you see what accounting systems actually read.
Convert to PDF
Save the rendered view as a PDF via your browser's print dialog, handy for filing and forwarding.
Plausibility checks
Flags missing mandatory fields and inconsistent totals (net + VAT ≠ gross, line sums).
No upload, works offline
All processing happens in your browser. There are no third-party requests at all, and after the first load the page even works offline.
Validate against official rules
Full business-rule validation (EN 16931 + German XRechnung CIUS) with a printable report, also fully local.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open an XRechnung / Factur-X file?

Drag the file into the area above or click “Choose file”. Double-clicking the XML itself usually opens a text editor full of angle brackets: that's what this viewer is for.

Is my invoice uploaded anywhere?

No. The page runs entirely in your browser and loads nothing from third-party servers. It's suitable for confidential invoices.

Why should I look at the XML inside a ZUGFeRD/Factur-X PDF?

Because for profiles at EN 16931 level, the embedded XML is the authoritative invoice content. Accounting software processes the data, not the PDF image. If they differ, you want to know.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Viewing, checking and printing are free, with no registration.
