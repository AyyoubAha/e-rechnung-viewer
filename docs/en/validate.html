What exactly is checked?

The file is validated against the official Schematron business rules published for EN 16931 (rule IDs like BR-…, BR-CO-…, BR-S-…) and — when the invoice declares the German XRechnung profile — additionally the German CIUS rules (BR-DE-…). This covers mandatory fields, total/VAT arithmetic, code list usage and profile-specific requirements. Transparency: XSD schema validation is not included; non-well-formed XML is rejected and structural problems typically surface as rule violations.

Why local instead of an online validator?

Invoices contain business data — amounts, bank details, customer names. Typical online validators upload your file to their servers. Here, rules and engine run in your browser; after the first load it even works offline.
